# Edge campaign methodology review - September 17, 2026

Scope: read-only review of the emerging four-family campaign, existing share diagnostic, exact-option replay, picker, news ledger and manual desk. This is a methodology review, not a performance result. Implementation was still in progress when inspected.

## Most direct risk

The dangerous transition is selecting a positive family/direction/symbol from the already observed August 3–September 16 sample, then describing its performance as independent confirmation. The initial SPY/QQQ/TSLA ORB diagnostic lost after costs in both partitions; those partitions are now consumed. Adding symbols or changing rules does not restore an unseen holdout. The new manifest correctly labels all historical results exploratory.

The second selection step matters equally: a directional result cannot be combined with a subsequently chosen affordable short-dated option and called an options strategy. Selecting expiry, strike, liquidity thresholds, time exits or reentry rules after inspecting returns creates a new experiment even when the underlying pattern name stays unchanged.

## Five concrete evidence gates

1. **Historical screen means research priority only.** Publish all four complete portfolio results and all rejected variants. Long/short and per-symbol results are diagnostics, not extra opportunities to declare a winner. The manifest's positive halves, double-cost, best-session removal and bootstrap checks are defensible conservative screening choices; they are not established statistical sufficiency. Thirty trades/fifteen active sessions are screening floors, not proof of an edge. A negative result can terminate or redesign a hypothesis; it must remain visible.

2. **Freeze before September 18 outcomes.** Persist the complete strategy and source/config hashes before its first prospective observation: universe, four primary portfolio comparisons, signal availability, next-observation entry delay, tie-breaking, exposure, costs, missing-data handling, exits, review window and permitted amendments. Also freeze option selection: call/put mapping, permitted expiries, delta/strike priority, spread/size gates, premium cap, fallback order and no-trade result. Register a fixed review endpoint before collection; do not stop the evaluation on the first profitable day. If no option selector is frozen and collecting by September 18, the options experiment begins at its actual later freeze time. Historical selection is allowed to choose a hypothesis for this test, but never becomes prospective evidence itself.

3. **Reconstruct the decision-time opportunity set.** Save the eligible listings and every candidate considered, including rejected/no-quote/unaffordable contracts, before selection. Retain expired contracts, contemporaneous symbol mappings, standard/adjusted deliverables and multipliers, source and receipt times, bid/ask and size, and timestamps or availability dates for Greeks, volume and open interest. The present picker refreshes only four shortlisted contracts per root; this is a bounded operational shortlist, not proof that it found the best contract in the chain. Define that shortlist algorithm as part of the strategy, save its inputs and rejection reasons, and never substitute today's chain in an old decision. Missing option data means unqualified/unresolved, not a profitable stock-return proxy.

4. **Measure the option implementation, including failures.** Record every qualifying signal, selected contract, skipped/cancelled/unfilled decision and exit, not only completed winners. Price entry from a later observed ask plus declared friction and exit from the observed bid minus friction; measure actual manual execution latency and fees when available. Test spreads, full premium loss, stop overshoot and correlated open premiums. A planned $25–$50 stop is not the maximum loss; long-option premium plus costs remains exposed. The current share-cost model and double-cost stress are not an option spread/slippage model. Null results from data gaps must not disappear from denominators or permit a completed-trades-only promotion claim. Quote replay, broker-paper fills and real manual fills remain separate evidence classes.

5. **Promote only the claim the evidence supports.** Shadow/paper research can start without a previously demonstrated edge; its purpose is to collect evidence. Calling an option setup qualified requires complete point-in-time selection, sufficient observations under the frozen rules, realistic costs, acceptable predeclared downside and review of execution failures. Report net dollars per session including zero-trade sessions, net dollars per trade, sample sizes, drawdown, full premium exposure, cost stress and concentration by session/symbol. Session-level uncertainty is preferable to treating correlated trades as independent. No universal trade count or bootstrap bound guarantees adequacy, and no current result establishes a win probability. A bounded manual-capital trial is a separate decision after this evidence exists, not an automatic consequence of a green historical screen.
